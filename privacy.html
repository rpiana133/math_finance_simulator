Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google Workspace, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use this only to identify you within the classroom simulator.
  • Portfolio Data: Your simulated trades, holdings, cash balance, and alert settings are stored in Google Cloud Storage and associated with your email address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the stock market simulation
  • To display your portfolio performance and classroom standings
  • To persist your data across sessions

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Storage (GCS) with encryption at rest. Access is restricted to the application service account and your classroom teacher.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for the duration of the course. Upon request by your teacher, your account and associated data will be deleted.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Workspace: Authentication only
  • Google Cloud Storage: Data persistence
  • Yahoo Finance (yfinance): Real-time and historical stock price data

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact your classroom instructor or the system administrator at St. John's School Guam.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be communicated through the application.
